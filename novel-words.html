Headline counts

A token is novel if that surface form never appears in the comparison training corpus. Types = unique novel words; tokens = how often those novel words occur in UK test. Tokenization: lowercase whitespace split (same as the Lengths page).

Comparison Novel types % of types Novel tokens % of tokens
UK test − UK train 602 33.7% 739 17.7%
UK test − AG train 200 11.2% 255 6.1%
UK test − both trains 160 8.9% 191 4.6%
Why AG covers more UK test words than UK train does: AG train is much larger (28,500 headlines, 38,838 word types) than UK train (1,216 headlines, 4,656 types), so many everyday English tokens in BBC test already appear in AG — even when UK-specific names and politics terms do not. Token rates are lower than type rates because novel words are often rare.
